My name is Fionn Mowlds and this is my very first blog.

I am an 18 year old athlete/college student studying Immersive Software Engineering at the University of Limerick. This first blog post is to discuss my love for computers, prevalent throughout my own life.

## My first experience with computers

My first experience with computers was with my own grandfather. He is a highly intelligent and capable man, and I remember from a very young age playing with old circuits with him and being taught how powerful electrical circuits can be. He himself was a civil engineer... but he was much more than that. From the young age of 14, <span class="point-highlight">he built his own radio system</span> <span class="point-arrow">&larr; that's him</span>! Not only did he figure out what he needed to build a fully working radio, he had to get an import licence and import all of the required pieces of technology into Cork in order to build it... at the age of 14!
